# Code of Conduct

Minion Yuri is a NASA Space Apps 2026 project built by a small team, open to
anyone who wants to look at the sky with us. We want working on it to feel
good for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to astronomy or to code.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Say when you're stuck (see the README: *blocked for 30+ minutes? say so*).
  Asking is never a nuisance.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people affected, and learn from them.
- Credit other people's work, including the data providers (NASA/IPAC IRSA,
  ESA Gaia, CDS).

## What we don't accept

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insulting or derogatory comments, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information (such as an address or email)
  without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Where this applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions), on the public
site, in the team group chat, and anywhere you represent the project, such as
at a Space Apps event.

## Reporting

If someone makes you feel unwelcome, tell a maintainer privately: message
**Raman (team owner)** or any other member of the [Team](README.md#team), or use
GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request.
Reports are kept confidential. Every report is looked at, and the people
handling it will step aside if they're involved.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide what's fair for the situation. In order of seriousness:

1. **Correction:** a private word explaining what was wrong.
2. **Warning:** a written warning, with consequences if it continues.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this Code of Conduct.
